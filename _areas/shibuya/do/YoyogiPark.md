Name: "Yoyogi Park"
Comments: "The park was the site of the 1964 Olympic Village and has been kept in great condition. Going from the crossing to this park will prove night and day difference as you see the tens of thousands of people in Shibuya and the thousands of shops packed into such a small space contrasted with the wide open park in the center of the biggest city in the world. The park is suitable for picnics, jogging, or even just sitting down to relax. There are ponds with fountains, a bird sanctuary and some gardens. On the other side of the park there is an outdoor stage and stadium where you’re sure to find an event on the weekend. For more about the park, read this: https://tokyocheapo.com/lifestyle/cheapo-guide-yoyogi-park/"
Price: "Free"
