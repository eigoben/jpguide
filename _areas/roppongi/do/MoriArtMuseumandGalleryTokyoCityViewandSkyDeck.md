Name: "Mori Art Museum and Gallery, Tokyo City View and Sky Deck"
Comments: "There is a great art museum with wonderful exhibits. Included in the price of the ticket is also the Tokyo City View, where you can view the city of Tokyo at 250m (52nd floor) from an indoor viewing room. For those of you that want to go even higher, there is an additional cost for Tokyo Sky Deck, which at 270m, is on the rooftop and is an outdoor viewing area of panoramic Tokyo."
