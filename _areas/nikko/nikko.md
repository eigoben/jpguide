Area_Description: "Located outside of the Tokyo Metropolitan area, and up north a few hours in Tochigi Prefecture lies the Nikko area. Nikko itself refers to city that houses the national park-esque forest and shrine area, but for this guide’s purpose, we’ll refer to it as everything around this area. Nikko should probably be one of the wonders of the world with its huge trees and beautifully designed early Japanese religious architecture, and it is one of the most sought after destinations in all of Japan, which says a lot when you realized that it’s a few hours from Tokyo and in the middle of nowhere."
