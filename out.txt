Area_Name: "Yokohama"
Area_Description: "Located about an hour south of central Tokyo you’ll find the bayside paradise known as Yokohama. The city sits right on the bay and has a modern feel. It is known for its upscale shops, a Chinatown district, bayside parks and amusement, and cup noodle museum. The compactness of the city makes it really easy to do a lot of things at once. You can see Chinatown in the morning, hit up a museum in the afternoon, walk near the bay in the evening, and enjoy the sunset from the top of a ferris wheel, and then head back to your hotel room to stare out at the lights of the city."
